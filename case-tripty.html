Tripty. An appetizing identity that ignites every dining sensation.

Transforming a high-energy culinary concept into a fully cohesive hospitality brand — spanning fire-branded takeout boxes, staff apparel, experiential dining room wall art, and citywide delivery vans.

Client
Tripty Restaurant Co.
Industry
Hospitality & Quick-Service Dining
Year & Location
2025 · Dhaka, Bangladesh
Disciplines
Brand Identity, Packaging, Spatial, Livery
The Vision

Translating heat, flavour, and culinary joy into visual energy.

Tripty is built on the philosophy of a "triptych of taste": sizzle, spice, and satisfaction. In a hyper-competitive dining market, restaurants that lack a distinct visual identity get treated as generic commodities on food delivery apps and street corners.

The Strategy: We built an exuberant, warm, and playful design system rooted in a grinning chef's hat, dynamic flame brushstrokes, and mouth-watering high-contrast typography. From the moment diners spot the delivery van to opening their takeout container, every touchpoint feels celebratory and crave-worthy.

Color & Typography

Spicy chili red, mustard amber, and deep kitchen charcoal.

Scientific studies confirm that vibrant red and warm gold stimulate appetite and induce feelings of culinary excitement. We balanced intense chili reds with a creamy milk-white ground and slate charcoal lines to prevent sensory fatigue.

The typography pairs a joyful, undulating script with bold condensed sans-serif headlines that command instant attention on outdoor posters and fast-scrolling food delivery apps.
