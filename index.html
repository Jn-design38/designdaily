Social Media Content Design

Designed for the feed.
Built to stop the thumb.

Feeds move at high velocity. Most brand content fails because it relies on standard templates that users scroll past on autopilot. We build social campaigns around bespoke art direction, cultural fluency, and deliberate contrast to command genuine attention.

01
Tripty Foods Appetite Storytelling Hook
The Design

Subverts standard food glamour shots with a messy, sauce-covered empty plate, bent cutlery, and deadpan copy (“If the plate could talk... it would ask for mercy”).

The Impact

Creates immediate emotional relatability that arrests food lovers mid-scroll, generating 3.4× higher organic comment engagement.

02
AVES Holdings Bilingual High-Ticket Lead Gen
The Design

Pairs architectural drone perspectives with heavyweight, balanced Bangla display typography and prominent 24-month EMI financing highlights.

The Impact

Bridges contemporary luxury aesthetics with native-language trust, driving qualified inbound WhatsApp inquiries from prime Banani property buyers.

03
AVES Holdings Lakefront Launch & Price Transparency
The Design

Anchors the lake view building with celebratory ribbon-cutting artwork, seismic engineering callouts, and upfront 8,500 TK/SQFT pricing.

The Impact

Radical price transparency pre-qualifies genuine buyers and establishes developer integrity before the first sales conversation.

04
SEOR AXIS Editorial Fashion & Product Anatomy
The Design

Juxtaposes minimalist apparel studio lifestyle photography with clean leathergoods product cutouts detailing security locks and pocket anatomy.

The Impact

Validates premium pricing through visible leather craftsmanship, converting casual fashion scrollers into high-AOV store visitors.
