07 / Before we begin Good questions

A few
details.

Clear expectations before we start. Here are answers to common questions about how the studio works.

What does “productized” mean here? +

A defined output, fixed fee, explicit sequence, realistic review limit, and predictable delivery window. It makes the purchase straightforward to evaluate and the work reliable to execute well.

Do you post the content for us? +

No. The studio delivers finished exports, copy captions, and organized source files. Daily posting, community management, and ad campaign setup remain with your team.

What do you need from my team? +

One primary decision-maker, verified product or service details, approved imagery or vector logos, and feedback gathered into one clear consolidated message per review round.

Can I start without a rebrand? +

Yes. The Paid Trial and Content 12 packages work directly with a usable existing identity. A full rebrand is never required to start working with our monthly content service.
