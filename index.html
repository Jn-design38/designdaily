The design process

Follow one brand from a blank page to the street.

This is how All Organics was made. Every project at Design Daily moves through the same six steps.

01 · BrainstormingFind the words first.

Wellness, fresh, pure, sustainable. We agree on what the brand must feel like before drawing anything.

02 · IdeationChoose a direction.

Moods and palettes are tested side by side. Cocoa and lime win: earthy, but never dull.

03 · SketchingGive the idea a shape.

Leaves, waves and swirls are drawn until a visual language appears that can stretch across anything.

04 · DigitisationMake it precise.

The mark is rebuilt in Illustrator, spaced, balanced and tested from favicon to billboard.

05 · DeliveryHand over real things.

Totes, cards, packs and posts arrive print-ready, with files named and sorted so anyone can use them.

06 · ResultsSee it on the road.

One consistent idea, recognisable on a truck, a shelf or a phone screen.
