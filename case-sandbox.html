Sandbox Lounge. Tactile hospitality branding with an earthy soul.

Crafting a sophisticated, grounded identity for an artisanal lounge and cocktail bar — marrying tactile sand textures, brutalist typography, warm amber lighting, and handcrafted stationery.

Client
Sandbox Lounge & Bar
Industry
Hospitality & Nightlife
Year & Location
2025 · Dhaka, Bangladesh
Disciplines
Brand Identity, Spatial Graphics, Menus
The Challenge

Moving beyond generic nightlife glitz.

Modern lounges often lean on neon cliches or sterile minimalism that feels disposable. Sandbox wanted to establish a slower, more deliberate sensory experience — a sanctuary where friends gather around textured stone tables, warm timber, and signature mixology.

The Strategy: We built an identity rooted in raw tactile elements: grain, sand, earth, and twilight embers. The typography balances confident editorial authority with handcrafted warmth, ensuring seamless harmony from the entrance signage to the cocktail coasters.

Identity Construction

A modern mark anchored in balance.

The Sandbox logo concept unites clean geometric balance with subtle architectural weight. The bespoke wordmark features custom kerning and optical refinements, engineered to be legible whether cast in bronze at the door or debossed onto textured leather menu covers.
