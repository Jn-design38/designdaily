Streetwear & Merch Case Study Apparel Design · Typographic Art · Hangtags · Lookbook

Goody Bro. High-voltage graphic streetwear and culture drops.

Developing a rebellious typographic apparel collection — bridging existential design statements ("Chaotic World", "Create Goodness", "Style vs Goodness"), screenprint separations, and collectible hangtags.

Goody Bro Streetwear
Industry

Disciplines

The Vision

Turning bold cultural commentary into wearable statements.

Streetwear is defined by perspective. T-shirts are not merely clothing; they are personal billboards that communicate belief, taste, and belonging. Generic clip-art or repetitive logo-slapping fails to resonate with digitally native youth audiences who demand authentic concept-driven graphics.

The Strategy: We built three conceptual capsule drops: "Chaotic World" (deconstructivist typography reflecting modern information overload), "Create Goodness" (dynamic positive rebellion), and "Style vs Goodness" (ironic cultural critique). Each graphic was designed specifically for oversized heavy-cotton drops with dedicated front and back lockups.

Color & Screenprint

High-contrast monochrome punctuated by acid neon.

The apparel collection relies on stark contrasts: deep volcanic blacks, high-density white discharge inks, and flashes of acid cyber lime and neon cyan. Every screen was separated to ensure maximum ink opacity and tactile texture when printed on 240+ GSM combed cotton.
