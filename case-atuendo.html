Atuendo. Architectural pattern language for high-fashion apparel.

Crafting an expressive identity system for an artisanal clothing label — combining precision geometric monogram construction, custom repeating brand patterns, and a warm Mediterranean earth palette.

Client
Atuendo Atelier
Industry
Contemporary Luxury Fashion
Year & Location
2024 · Spain & Global
Disciplines
Identity, Pattern Design, Stationery
The Vision

Textile geometry meets modern brand identity.

Atuendo translates to "attire" or "regal garment". The brand crafts structured modern apparel using natural linen, wool, and structured cottons. They needed a visual identity that felt as tailored as their garments — neither generic minimalist nor overly fast-fashion trendy.

The Strategy: We derived the entire visual system from the weave and structural lines of couture tailoring. The mark operates both as an iconic standalone monogram and as a generative geometric pattern that can be embossed into leather tags, printed on tissue, or woven into garment jacquards.

Color & Structure

Terracotta rust, ochre gold, and textured ecru.

The 3-tier color palette draws inspiration from Spanish architectural clay tiles, sun-drenched stucco, and raw unbleached linen. The warm earthy tones give the brand an unmistakable artisanal warmth that stands apart from standard black-and-white fashion labels.
