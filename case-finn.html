FINN. Quiet luxury engineered through obsessive packaging.

Crafting an architectural identity system for handcrafted premium leather goods — balancing rigid matte noir boxes, blind debossed envelopes, and a singular crimson signature thread.

Client
FINN Leathercraft
Industry
Luxury Accessories & Goods
Year & Location
2025 · Dhaka / Global
Disciplines
Packaging, Stationery, Monogram
The Challenge

Creating an unboxing ritual that commands premium pricing.

Handcrafted leather products demand a packaging experience that matches the precision of genuine full-grain leather. Standard retail boxes felt disposable and diminished the perceived value of the product before the client even touched the leather.

The Strategy: We stripped away decorative ornament and replaced it with tactile architecture: heavyweight matte charcoal stocks, clean geometric bevels, blind embossed monogram patterns, and surgical foil registration. Every box feels like a keepsake worth preserving on a collector's shelf.

Materials & Palette

Subtle contrasts, deep blacks, and a crimson focal point.

The FINN monogram is a bold, modern geometric typographic form. We developed a proprietary brand pattern repeating the monogram at micro-scale to create texture on internal envelope sleeves and unboxing tissue.

A single flash of crimson red acts as the functional seal — directing the customer’s eye to where the box unlatches.
