All Organics. Organic food with trust built into every touchpoint.

How we shaped an unmistakable visual language for Bangladesh’s conscious organic food category — spanning consumer packaging, urban billboards, retail canvas totes, and electric delivery trucks.

Client
All Organics Ltd.
Industry
Organic Food & Wellness
Year & Location
2025 · Dhaka, Bangladesh
Disciplines
Identity, Packaging, Livery
The Challenge

Cutting through the noise of crowded supermarket shelves.

The organic food landscape in Bangladesh was overwhelmed with rustic kraft textures, cliché green leaves, and inconsistent packaging that failed to convey premium quality or clinical trust. Consumers were hesitant about paying premium rates without visual proof of superior standards.

The Objective: Build an identity that feels authentically organic yet razor-sharp, modern, and trustworthy. The brand needed to hold up on a supermarket shelf, on the side of a city delivery van, and on digital feeds with immediate distinction.

The System

A leaf held inside an unbroken circle.

The core emblem unites two eternal symbols: the seed/leaf of pure cultivation, nestled within an unbroken circle representing cycle, sustainability, and complete protection. The line weight is carefully calibrated so the mark remains legible down to a 12mm bottle cap or embroidered apron.

We paired the monogram with a custom-tracked geometric sans-serif that speaks with clarity, quiet authority, and warmth.
