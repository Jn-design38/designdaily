Food Packaging & Brand Identity Identity · Retail Packaging · Gable Boxes · Pouch Design · Outdoor

Bhumi. Heritage agricultural grains reimagined for modern retail.

Engineering an authoritative packaging architecture for heritage rice products — featuring a stalk-of-paddy 'B' monogram, distinctive color-coded pouches, and structural gable boxes for retail shelves.

Client
Bhumi Agro Food Ltd.
Industry
Heritage Food & Agriculture
Year & Location
2025 · Bangladesh
Disciplines
Brand Identity, Packaging Suite, Out of Home
The Challenge

Standing apart on aisles filled with generic plastic grain sacks.

Traditional rice packaging in South Asia is notoriously indistinguishable: low-grade transparent polybags or generic yellow sacks that treat premium heritage grains as bulk commodities. Consumers were unable to differentiate between ordinary milled rice and fragrant aromatic heirloom grains.

The Strategy: We established a distinctive shelf presence using rich forest jade, golden harvest accents, and crisp product family differentiation. Each format — from 5kg standalone Kataribhog rice sacks to fine rice flour pouches and dessert gift boxes — carries the same aristocratic family DNA.

The Mark & Palette

A stalk of paddy born from the letter 'B'.

The monogram seamlessly fuses the uppercase Latin 'B' with the graceful curve of a ripe paddy stalk. The upper bowl represents the leaf; the lower bowl holds the grain. The mark is accompanied by both English and Bangla wordmarks to ensure instant recognition across regional and metropolitan markets.
